本期传导判断

Current pass-through

内存是 2026 年消费电子 BOM 的首要上行压力，尤其影响中高端手机与电脑；品牌更可能先压缩毛利或调整配置，再上调零售价。

Memory is the leading 2026 BOM pressure for consumer electronics. Brands are more likely to protect price architecture through mix and margin before raising retail price.

  • 高敏感：手机、笔记本、游戏设备
  • High sensitivity: phones, notebooks, gaming
  • 中敏感：TV、智能家居、配件
  • Medium: TV, smart home, accessories
